Anticoagulation research toolkit

Warfarin INR workbench

Target-range interpretation, manually specified percentage arithmetic, and interpolated time in therapeutic range (TTR). All calculations run in your browser.

Research and education only — not a prescribing tool. The workbench does not determine dose changes, hold instructions, bridging, vitamin K, or emergency reversal. For bleeding or markedly abnormal INR, follow an appropriate clinical escalation pathway.
